Step 1 — audio mix (mix.wav):
ffmpeg -i voice.wav -i <SFX>/music/bgm_calm.ogg -i <SFX>/sfx/ding.ogg -i <SFX>/sfx/transition_swoosh.ogg -filter_complex
"[0:a]aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[voice];
[1:a]volume='if(between(t,0,3.58),0.11,if(between(t,3.58,17.22),0.11,if(between(t,17.22,30.69),0.12,if(between(t,30.69,45.52),0.18,if(between(t,45.52,46.03),0.0,0.11)))))':eval=frame,atrim=0:52.36,aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[bgm];
[2:a]adelay=30690:all=1,volume=0.5,aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[ding];
[3:a]asplit=3[sw0][sw1][sw2];
[sw0]adelay=3580:all=1,volume=0.2,aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[s1];
[sw1]adelay=17220:all=1,volume=0.2,aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[s2];
[sw2]adelay=30690:all=1,volume=0.2,aformat=sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[s3];
[voice][bgm][ding][s1][s2][s3]amix=inputs=6:duration=longest:normalize=0,loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11,alimiter=limit=0.7:level=false[mix]"
-map "[mix]" -ar 48000 mix.wav

Step 2 — mux (v004.mp4):
ffmpeg -i raw.mp4 -i mix.wav -filter_complex "[0:v]ass=captions.ass[vout]"
-map "[vout]" -map 1:a -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 30
-c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest -movflags +faststart v004.mp4

Notes:
- BGM envelope: hook 0.11 / beat1 0.11 / beat2 0.12 / beat3 0.18 / 0.0 in the
  0.5s pre-payoff silence (45.52-46.03) / payoff 0.11. Voice raw -18.50 LUFS,
  so BGM lands ~20-24 dB below voice.
- Ding 0.5 at 30.69s (girl appears). Swoosh 0.2 at 3.58/17.22/30.69 (was 0.4;
  the swoosh file peaks at -3.7 dB raw and hit the alimiter ceiling).
- alimiter limit=0.7:level=false (level=true is auto-gain, WRONG). No apad;
  amix duration=longest (duration=first hangs). -shortest on the mux
  (voice 52.36s < raw video 53.03s).
- Full script: assemble_v004.sh (same directory).
