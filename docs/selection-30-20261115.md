# 30-video deep-analysis selection — faceless Shorts channel (2026-11-15)

Engineer selection (Research never claimed queue task 5246f45f). Basis: the 100 verified candidates (task ac56d158, verified by a1d31407), 92 downloaded + analysed on this VPS, scored with ChatGPT's 100-point formula (hook 3s 25%, retention 20%, faceless feasibility 20%, payoff 15%, production speed 10%, audience fit 5%, copyright/originality 5%; total = 5H+4R+4F+3P+2C+A+L) and Gemini's weighted filters (no-camera 40%, duration 25%, 3s hook 20%, outlier 15%).

## How the scoring was done (honest limits)

- **Frames actually viewed:** 4 frames per video (1s = hook window, 25%, 50%, 75%) for 49 videos, as 2x2 contact sheets generated with ffmpeg from the downloaded files. FB-05's real 0/1/2s hook frames were viewed (they exist in its analysis folder).

- **What could NOT be judged:** the spoken hook and the audio (no transcript/ASR on this VPS; the 30s audio wav exists per video but was not listened to). Where the 1s frame shows no text and no clear visual hook, the hook is recorded as unknown and scored low — nothing was invented from a title.

- **Scene keyframes** in summary.json are scene-change frames only (the 92-video batch predates the 0/1/2s hook-frame fix), so keyframe counts are a pacing proxy, not a hook record. Text-only changes (stickman cards, list reveals) do not trigger scene detection, so kf=0 does not mean 'static' for text-driven videos.

- **No-camera filter (hard):** a video is selected only if its mechanism can be remade without a camera. Big-production/camera content (MrBeast, Zach King, Daniel LaBelle, Khaby Lame, etc.) is hook/retention reference only and is NOT in the 30. Sketch/dialogue videos are included only with a full re-animation remake format, and their faceless score reflects that cost.

- **Duration:** only 13 of the 92 analysed videos are in the 38-65s target window, so duration is scored as a fit criterion, not a hard gate. Short videos (10-25s) are selected when their format extends to 40-50s (e.g. a fact list with more facts); long videos (80-105s) are selected when the format cuts down (e.g. a 105s story cut to 60s). The remake note says which.

- **Thresholds:** ChatGPT's 85+ = deep analysis, 70-84 = compare, <70 = skip. Only 3 of the 30 reach 85+ (#50: 89, #81: 87, #80: 87). Mean of the 30 is 68.3. The pool is capped by the no-camera filter (most humor/surprise videos are camera sketches) and by copyright (many are ripped TV/meme clips, L=2). The scores are honest, not inflated to hit the threshold.

- **Niche-count discrepancy (resolved):** ChatGPT flagged the niche counts as summing to 98, not 100. Recount from the candidate list: 24+17+15+10+10+9+5+4+4+1+1 = 100. The two singleton niches ('emotional everyday life stories' 1, 'illogical creative' 1) were dropped in ChatGPT's addition. There are NO unclassified videos.
